## Scope
Public site (home, Today) and its app flow (sign-in, quiz, résumé, build, report, plans). Mode: Persuade for home; Operate for the app flow. V2 (user request, 2026-10-21): HR console (/enterprise) now joins the same world and stops being dense: roomy cards, icon stats, 16px base, following cluely.com's inner pages (pricing-style cards on a soft sky).

## Audience and job
Individual learners and employees on a phone or laptop: understand in seconds what AiversePulse does, pick one of three ways in (quiz, résumé, pick a job), and read every app screen without squinting.

## Direction contract
THESIS: Few words at a readable size. The page refuses the dense SaaS landing with eyebrow kickers, 01/02/03 numbers, chip pills and floating fake-UI labels; one big serif line, one sentence, one action per block.
OWN-WORLD: User-pinned to cluely.com's world: white ground, one clear blue (oklch 0.53 0.19 263) plus a saturated blue field for the lead card, pale blue-grey cards, EB Garamond 500 headlines over Geist UI text, pill buttons, 32px-radius cards, the JobSmart owl recoloured blue as the only character. No background picture (user choice). V2: icons lead every destination and status (icon + 1-2 word label, user choice); liquid glass only on floating layers (header bar, tab bar, Start menu, composer, sheets, drawers, HR top bar; user choice), over a soft blue sky gradient; every job shows a drawn job-family icon tile coloured by ISCO major group; the three core functions live in a Start menu reachable from every page.
STORY: The visitor sees the owl and "Where could your skills take you?", learns the promise (roles + a 90-day plan you approve, every claim cited), and starts the quiz, uploads a résumé or picks a job.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Centred column: owl 168px, h1 96px serif in three lines, one 21px sentence, a blue "Start Quick Match" pill with two outline pills beside it; the top of the two big cards just below the fold.
FORM: User-pinned direction. The user wrote: "the ui need follow this style cuz the ui has problem it has many small words u can refer to this https://cluely.com/". No concept roll ran, so there is no seed reference (seed: none, direction pinned by user).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
